Fight Club

Year: 1999

Director: David Fincher

Writers: Jim Uhls

Stars: Brad Pitt, Edward Norton, Helena Bohnam Carter

Awards: Academy Awards 2000 Nominee

Cast

  • Edward Norton as Narrator
  • Brad Pitt as Tyler Durden
  • Helena Bohnam Carter as Marla Singer
  • Jared Leto as Angel Face
  • Robert Paulsen as Meat Loaf
  • Eion Bailey as Ricky
  • Richard Chesler Zach Grenier

My Review

  1. The direction and cinematography is amazing, rewatching this movie makes you notice the little snippets that you don't notice on the first watch
  2. The movie has many themes like materialism, corporate work environment, mascuulinity, depression and the movie explored them brilliantly
  3. Brad Pitt, Edward Norton and Helena Bonham Carter's performances in particular were spectacular
  4. The plot twist by the end of the movie is great and I did not see it coming the first time I watched it
  5. The movie has cemented itself into pop culture history. The line about rules about fight club is recognizable by any movie enthusiast
